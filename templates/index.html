    How ScanScope Operates

    Deterministic Routing

    Routes inputs strictly to dedicated architectures. Histopathology patches map to ResNet-18 (Camelyon16), while thoracic radiographs route to Chest ResNet-18.

    Modality Preprocessing

    Images undergo 224×224 bilinear scaling, 3-channel alignment, and ImageNet standardization to align with neural layer input bounds.

    Privacy & Security

    Inference occurs locally on server CPU. Uploaded buffers are wiped from RAM and volatile storage immediately after inference.
